How the app evaluates a site

  1. Legal. Zoning makes each sector allowed, conditional (score ×0.85), or prohibited.
  2. Physical. Parcel area is calculated from the boundary digitized on the map, and distances are measured from its centroid. Zoning must allow buildable floor area: the lower of parcel area × FAR and parcel area × BCR × max floors. The app shows a warning when this is less than half the weighted median comparable GFA.
  3. Financial. Residual land value = project value − development cost − developer profit. A sector passes when the result is positive.
  4. Maximum productivity. The sector with the highest residual land value per m² among those that pass.

The recommendation uses a combined score from market performance, supply headroom, transit access, activity centers, and land value. Each comparable is weighted 1 ÷ (1 + distance ÷ 800 m), so closer properties have more influence on price, occupancy, vacant space, NLA per room, and building size. Each sector's GFA follows the weighted median NLA of comparables within the radius, divided by efficiency, then limited by FAR and max floors. A sector with fewer than 3 comparables within the radius is not evaluated. This method does not yet measure how much new space the market can absorb, so the app shows a warning when occupancy is below 60% or vacant space is above 30%.

Apartment project value uses strata sales. Hotel, office, and retail use net operating income divided by the cap rate. The model does not yet include timing, construction interest, or land acquisition cost.
